LINKSCOPE V1 - INTERNAL LINKING AUTOMATION

1. Download and unzip this package.
2. Create a NEW GitHub repository: linkscope-internal-linking
3. Upload the INDIVIDUAL files from this extracted folder, not the ZIP.
   Keep app.py, internal_link_engine.py, reporting.py,
   requirements.txt and fictional_demo_pages.csv at the root.
4. Open https://share.streamlit.io/ and create a second Streamlit app:
   Repository: YOUR-USERNAME/linkscope-internal-linking
   Branch: main
   Main file: app.py
5. Once it is live, choose Fictional demo > Analyze fictional example.
6. Download the Excel audit under Downloads & method.

DO NOT upload confidential client data without permission.
For live website crawling, configure CRAWL_ACCESS_KEY in Streamlit
Manage app > Settings > Secrets. See README.md for details.
